Daily Log

A construction daily log app for your iPhone.

A construction daily log app replaces the paper site diary: record progress, crew and per-activity progress each day, with photos and notes attached. Projomia’s Daily Log works offline on iPhone, is free, and keeps every entry in a local library on your phone, so the record is there when a question comes up later.

What a daily log app should record

  • Progress. What was done today, by activity and area.
  • Crew on site. Who was there and for which work.
  • Per-activity progress. How far each activity moved, so slippage shows early.
  • Evidence. Photos, drawings and notes linked to the entry.
  • Blockers and decisions. What is waiting, why, and who needs to act.

For a full field list (weather, deliveries, inspections, safety, sign-off), use the free construction daily report template.

Daily log, site diary, daily report: the same job

“Site diary” is the usual name in the UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand. “Daily log” and “daily report” are common in North America and the Middle East, and site engineers in India often call it the DPR (daily progress report). The app does the same job whatever you call it.

How the Projomia Daily Log works

  1. Plan. Add activities, areas and decisions in Planner.
  2. Log the day. Record daily progress, crew on site and progress per activity in the Daily Log.
  3. Attach the evidence. Capture photos into the project and room, and keep notes beside them.
  4. Write it down. Use the Journal for anything that does not fit a field.
  5. See what needs you. “Today’s attention” shows items flagged blocked, follow-up or decide, each linked to its evidence.

Built for site engineers and contractors

Projomia is for the people who run and record the work: site managers, site engineers, project managers and contractors. It suits individuals and small teams who want one dependable daily record on their own phone.

Works offline on site

Capture, notes, Planner, Journal and Daily Log all work without signal. The library stays in the app on your iPhone, with no Projomia account or server in this release. Read more about Projomia as an offline construction app.

Questions

What is a construction daily log app?

An app that replaces the paper site diary: you record each day’s progress, crew and issues on your phone, with photos and notes attached.

Is there a free site diary app for iPhone?

Yes. Projomia is free on iPhone (iOS 18 or later) with no subscription, ads or tracking, and its Daily Log works as a site diary.

Can I write daily logs offline?

Yes. Projomia’s Daily Log, Planner, Journal and photo capture all work without signal and save to a local library on the phone.

Do I need AI to use the Daily Log?

No. AI is optional and off until you connect your own OpenAI API key or ChatGPT sign-in. AI replies stay unverified until you confirm them.

Is a site diary the same as a daily log?

Yes. Site diary, daily log, daily report and DPR are regional names for the same daily record of work on site.
