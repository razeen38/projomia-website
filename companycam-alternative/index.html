A free CompanyCam alternative that works offline.

If you want a free CompanyCam alternative that works offline and needs no account, Projomia keeps site photos, notes and daily logs on your iPhone with no subscription. CompanyCam is a paid cloud platform built for sharing photos across a team. Projomia suits engineers and small contractors who want a private, local record instead.

When CompanyCam is the better fit

If several people need to see the same photos in real time, from the office and the field, a team cloud platform is the right tool. CompanyCam is built for that, and it also captures offline and syncs later.

When Projomia is the better fit

  • You want it free. No subscription, ads or tracking. Optional AI uses your own OpenAI connection, billed by OpenAI.
  • You work without signal. Everything saves to the phone first.
  • You want your records to stay yours. No account and no Projomia server; the library lives in the app on your iPhone.
  • You need more than photos. Notes beside each photo, a Daily Log with crew and per-activity progress, a Planner and “Today’s attention” for what is blocked.

Questions

Is there a free version of CompanyCam?

CompanyCam’s pricing page offers a free trial and paid plans; it does not list a free plan (checked 27 November 2026).

What is a free alternative to CompanyCam?

Projomia is free on iPhone with no subscription or account and works offline. Fieldwire, SiteCam and Timemark offer free plans with limits if you need team cloud sharing.

Which construction photo app works without signal?

Projomia saves photos, notes and daily logs to a local library on the phone without signal. CompanyCam, SiteCam and Timemark capture offline and upload later.

Can I use Projomia with my team?

Projomia has Project Team labels, but no cloud sharing in this release. Records stay on the phone; you can copy or export the library whenever you choose.
