Offline construction photo apps for iPhone, compared.

The best offline construction photo app for iPhone depends on whether you need team cloud sharing or a private record on your phone. Projomia is free on iPhone, keeps photos, notes and daily logs in a local library with no account, and works fully offline. Cloud apps such as CompanyCam capture offline and sync to a team account.

What “works offline” really means

Almost every construction photo app can take a picture without signal. The differences are in what happens next:

  • Offline capture, then sync. Photos are saved on the phone and upload to the vendor’s cloud when you are back online. Good for teams who need everyone to see the same photos.
  • Offline-first, local library. Photos, notes and logs live on the phone and are never uploaded by the app. Good for individuals and small teams who want a private, dependable record and no account.
  • Offline viewing. Whether plans, past photos and reports are readable on site without signal. Check this if you work in basements, plant rooms or remote sites.

The apps

Projomia

Projomia is a free iPhone app that files each photo into a project and room, keeps notes beside it, and adds a Planner, Journal, Daily Log and inspection checklists by location. Everything stays in a local library on the phone, with no Projomia account or server. AI is optional and uses your own OpenAI connection. There is no cloud team sharing in this release.

CompanyCam

CompanyCam is a paid photo platform for contractor teams, with a free trial. Its pricing page says photos can be captured on site without service and sync when you are back online. Each seat is one person with their own login, and it runs on iOS and Android. It suits teams that want every photo shared in one cloud account.

Fieldwire

Fieldwire is a construction management app with plans, tasks and photos. Its free Basic plan covers up to 5 users, 3 projects and 100 sheets, with paid plans per user. The App Store listing mentions an offline mode and an offline plan viewer. It suits teams that work from drawings and need shared task tracking.

SiteCam

SiteCam is a construction photo and report app with a free plan (3 projects, 500 photos, 25 reports, unlimited team members) and a paid Pro plan. Its pricing page lists offline support in the mobile apps, plus a web app. It suits small teams that want shared photo reports without paying at first.

Timemark

Timemark is a photo-proof camera that stamps time and location on photos. Its free plan covers 100 photos and 3 projects, with paid plans from $5 per user per month. The App Store listing says it captures photos with full metadata without signal and uploads them automatically later. It suits crews who need stamped photo proof.

Which one fits you?

  • Site engineer or solo contractor who wants a private record: an offline-first app with a local library and no account, such as Projomia.
  • Small team that needs to share photos: a cloud app with a free tier, such as SiteCam, Fieldwire or Timemark.
  • Contractor company standardising photo documentation: a paid team platform such as CompanyCam.

Questions

Can I take construction photos without internet on iPhone?

Yes. All five apps on this page save photos on the phone without signal. Cloud apps upload them later; Projomia keeps them in a local library on the iPhone and never uploads them.

Do offline construction photo apps need an account?

Most cloud apps ask you to sign up so photos can sync to a team account. Projomia has no account or server in this release.

Is there a free construction photo app with no subscription?

Projomia is free on iPhone with no subscription, ads or tracking. Fieldwire, SiteCam and Timemark offer free plans with limits, and CompanyCam offers a free trial.

Where are offline photos stored?

Cloud apps store photos on the phone until they can upload them to the vendor’s servers. Projomia stores them in a local library in the app on your iPhone.

Android is in early access. Email support@projomia.com with your Google Play email to request it. Android has no AI and no sync.
