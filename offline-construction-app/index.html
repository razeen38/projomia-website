Offline construction app

An offline construction app that keeps the whole record on your phone.

Projomia is a free iPhone app that keeps site photos, drawings, notes, plans and daily logs together in a local library on your phone. Capture, Planner, Journal and Daily Log all work without signal. There is no Projomia account and no Projomia cloud server. AI is optional and uses your own OpenAI connection.

Why offline matters on site

Basements, plant rooms, rural sites and new structures often have weak or no signal. An app that needs a connection to save a photo or a note loses the record at the moment it matters. Projomia saves everything to the phone first, so the record is complete whether you are online or not.

What works without a connection

  • Capture. Take photographs into the project and room you choose, or import images, drawings and documents with the photo picker.
  • Notes beside the evidence. Write the note next to the photograph it explains, and browse by project, folder and date.
  • Planner. Record activities, areas and decisions.
  • Journal and Daily Log. Log daily progress, crew on site and progress per activity.
  • Inspection checklists by location. Work through checks room by room.
  • Today’s attention. See what is blocked, what needs a follow-up and what needs a decision, each linked to its evidence.

Where your records live

The library is stored in the app on your iPhone. This release has no Projomia login, no Projomia cloud account and no Projomia server, so your project records are not uploaded anywhere by Projomia. Copy or export the library whenever you choose. The privacy policy explains the details.

Optional AI, only when you ask

AI is off until you connect your own OpenAI API key or sign in with ChatGPT. It needs a connection only when you send a request, and it uses only what that request authorizes. Replies are saved as unverified and do not change the project until you confirm them. OpenAI bills any API usage directly; Projomia has no subscription.

Who it is for

Site managers, site engineers, project managers and contractors who want one dependable record of the work: what happened, what is waiting, and why. It suits small teams and individuals who prefer to keep project records on their own device.

Get Projomia

Projomia is free on iPhone (iOS 18 or later). Android is in early access: email support@projomia.com with the Google account email you use on Google Play. The Android app works offline but does not include AI. A Mac companion is not available yet.

Questions

Does Projomia work without internet?

Yes. Capture, notes, documents, Planner, Journal and Daily Log work without a network. Only optional AI requests need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. This release has no Projomia account, login or cloud server. Your library stays in the app on your phone.

Where are my site photos stored?

In the Projomia library on your iPhone. Import uses the photo picker, so Projomia does not read your whole photo library.

Is Projomia free?

Yes. The iPhone app is free with no subscription, ads or tracking. If you choose to use AI with your own OpenAI API key, OpenAI bills that usage.

Is there an Android version?

Android is in early access. Email support@projomia.com with your Google Play account email to request it. Android works offline but has no AI.
